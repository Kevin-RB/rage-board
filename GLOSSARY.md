# Rage Board

A single, highly visible to-do page that visibly breaks down as its owner's workload outgrows their workday, so coworkers can see the overload.

## Language

**Board**:
The single page holding the Tasks and everything that happens to them.
_Avoid_: List, app, page

**Task**:
An item of requested work, with a title and an Estimate.
_Avoid_: To-do, item, ticket

**Estimate**:
The time a Task is expected to take.
_Avoid_: Duration, time

**Shift**:
The owner's workday length, which they set themselves.
_Avoid_: Workday, window

**Overload**:
The total of all Estimates divided by the Shift, expressed as a percentage.
_Avoid_: Load, rage level

**Meltdown**:
The full breakdown of the Board once Overload passes 100%.
_Avoid_: Explosion, break, crash
